Description:
Navi is a navigator for driving in Ukraine. Search an address, see a route that follows streets, and go. Regional maps stay on the phone, so you keep the map when the network drops.

What it does
• Search streets, house numbers, places, and a point from the clipboard
• Routes on roads, not through yards and buildings
• House numbers on the map when you zoom in
• Spoken turn guidance
• Home, work, and saved places in one tap
• Download a region: Odesa, Kyiv, Lviv, and others
• Guidance when GPS is weak, using phone sensors and optional OBD-II speed

Online, Navi uses live routing and search. Offline, you keep the downloaded region, saved house numbers, and the base road network.

No account. No ads. The trip track is not sent to the developer. History and maps stay on your iPhone.

Navi does not drive the car. Signs, lights, and traffic rules come first.
